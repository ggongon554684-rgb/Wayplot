## Cross-cutting findings

1. **Tier visibility:** [MainShellPresenter.cs](../WayPlot/Presenters/MainShellPresenter.cs) includes Data Collection, Main Transaction, and Deals & Payments tabs for Tier A. The Intelligence tab is still shown but disabled with an “Available in Tier B” hint; it is not hidden. There is no Branching tab. Commissions is shown to tenant users and is a Tier A feature. CSV import is reached from Contacts; the former standalone Import placeholder tab is no longer in the shell.
2. **Customer money summary:** The Contacts screen shows contact identity/source/consent only. It does not show the documented per-deal parcel, balance, next due date, or Current/Overdue summary. Balance and payment history are available separately in Deals & Payments, but next due/overdue calculations and installment schedules are not represented in the current flow.
3. **Cross-Agent visibility:** **Yes.** `ContactService.ListAsync` and `LeadService.ListAsync` have no caller/role filtering; the Contacts and Leads screens therefore include other Agents' contacts and leads. `LeadService.GetFileAsync` and interaction listing also lack caller scope, and the parcel map loads parcels and each parcel's leads without Agent scoping. The Deals list and payment reads do enforce Agent ownership in `DealService`, so another Agent's deal does not appear there; however, the same customer's/contact and lead data can be seen through Contacts, Leads, and the parcel map.
4. **Service-side role enforcement is uneven.** Deal and Commission operations have explicit role checks. CSV import checks Agent/Tenant Admin. Parcel, contact capture/list, lead attach/list/file/stage movement, and interaction logging/listing do not enforce the actor's role in their domain services. UI visibility alone does not provide equivalent service protection.

## Prioritized remaining Tier A work

1. **Close service authorization and privacy gaps:** scope Agent contacts, leads, lead files, interactions, and parcel-map lead data to ownership/assignment; add service role checks for parcel/contact/lead/interaction mutations and Tenant Admin-only assignment/reassignment.
2. **Finish parcel and lead capture workflows:** add Designer-based parcel create/edit and photo management, owner details, assignment/review, and a coherent contact/source/referrer-to-parcel lead capture flow.
3. **Complete contact and customer money views:** add contact detail/edit and per-deal balance/payment summary; implement installment terms, next due date, and Current/Overdue calculation.
4. **Complete close/cancel/default lifecycle:** make close confirmation update parcel/contact status as specified; add default/refund handling, parcel-state recalculation, and cancellation/default commission handling.
5. **Add remaining Tenant Admin capabilities:** company profile, staff/role management, configurable pipeline stages, and lead/parcel reassignment.
6. **Implement deal documents and landowner payouts:** add service/repository operations and Designer-based screens for document attachment and payout recording/history.
7. **Implement communication delivery and offline sync:** outbound SMS/email (with consent enforcement) and a real pending-change synchronization path; logging alone and a Pending sync flag do not complete these use cases.
